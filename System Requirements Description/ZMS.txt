Our customer is the visitor admissions manager of a private zoo.The zoo has bought a small turnstile system,consisting of a rotating barrier and a coin-accepting device,that can be connected to two ports of a small computer.Our job is to build the software to operate the turnstile system.The requirement is,essentially,in two parts.First,no visitor should be able to enter the zoo without having paid the entry price(which is two coins).Second,any visitor who has paid the two coins should be allowed to enter.
