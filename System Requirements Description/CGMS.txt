A continuous glucose monitor (CGM) is a wearable device used to help patients continuously monitor their blood glucose levels and automatically manage insulin injections. A timer in the monitor periodically reminds the controller to monitor the patient's blood glucose. Based on the timing information, the controller controls the sensor to automatically sample the patient's blood glucose. A glucose calculator calculates the blood glucose concentration and sends it to the controller, which then controls the display device to show the blood glucose concentration and receive the insulin dosage input by the doctor. The controller sends an injection command to the pump, which delivers the corresponding dose of insulin to the syringe. The syringe then administers the insulin injection to the patient according to this dosage.
